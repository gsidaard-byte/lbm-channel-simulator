Conclusion

The two-plate architecture cannot be tuned into taming the Re-4724 jet: searched with a real solver, the resolved-scale flow still punches through (see the flow-field views). Next steps are structural: a third plate close behind the vane cascade, a deeper jet-splitting cascade, or the coat-hanger header distributor — and optimization runs at the confirmation mesh (overnight budget).
